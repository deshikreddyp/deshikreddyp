Deshik Reddy Putluru

I am a Ph.D. candidate developing high-fidelity finite-element models for multiphysics systems. My research uses numerical simulations to understand the biomechanics of cerebrospinal fluid flow and drug delivery in the spinal canal. Alongside these high-fidelity models, I build reduced-order models and am developing scientific machine learning surrogates to make patient-specific simulations practical for clinical and pharmaceutical decisions.
